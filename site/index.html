Is the PHP in your image fixed?

Every day, whichphp reads the PHP inside popular container images, without running them, and checks it against the PHP2Shell fixes for CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619. Distro backports and end-of-life branches are taken into account.

What a verdict doesn't prove

  • It covers the image as published. A container you started earlier keeps its old image until you recreate it, and recreating it erases its logs, so save those first.
  • Runtime settings can change session.upload_progress.enabled. Until PHP fixes CVE-2026-115560, turning it off server-wide is the only mitigation.
  • "Fixed" says nothing about whether a system was compromised before it was fixed.

About the data

Images are read with Syft and matched with Grype; whichphp adds the PHP-specific rules. Nothing is executed and no traffic is sent to anyone's servers.

Wrong verdict? Report it with evidence. Missing an image? Suggest it.
