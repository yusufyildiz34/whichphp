What a verdict does not prove

  • It covers the image as published. Your running container may be older; recreate it to pick up a rebuilt tag.
  • Runtime settings (environment variables, mounted config files) can change session.upload_progress.enabled. Until PHP fixes CVE-2026-115560, turning it off server-wide is the mitigation.
  • "Fixed" says nothing about whether a system was compromised before it was fixed.
